# Delete your Qibla and Mosque Explorer account

**App:** Qibla and Mosque Explorer (Game-On Creation)

## Option 1: In the app (instant)
1. Open the app and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete my account**, then **Delete**.
3. Pick your Google account when asked. Your account and coins are deleted right away.

## Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)
1. Send an email to **gameoncreation@gmail.com** from the Google account you signed in with.
2. Subject: **Delete my Qibla Explorer account**
3. We delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted
- Your sign in record (name, email, profile picture link, user ID)
- All coins and purchase records stored in your account

## What is kept
- Nothing is kept by us after deletion.
- Google Play keeps its own payment records, as required by law, under Google's privacy policy.
- Deleting your account does not refund purchases. Unused coins are lost.
