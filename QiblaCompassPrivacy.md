# Privacy Policy: Qibla and Mosque Explorer

**Developer:** Game-On Creation  
**Contact:** gameoncreation@gmail.com  
**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Qibla and Mosque Explorer app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. Location
- The app uses your phone's location to show the Qibla direction, prayer times and nearby mosques, prayer spaces, halal food and groceries.
- Prayer times and the Qibla are calculated on your phone.
- To find nearby places, the app sends your approximate coordinates to map services (Geoapify and OpenStreetMap Overpass servers). They are used only to answer that search. We do not store your location on our servers.
- If you pick a city by hand, the city name is looked up the same way.
- You can deny location permission and still pick a city by hand.

## 2. Google sign in (optional)
- You can use the app as a guest. Signing in is optional.
- If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture link and a user ID from Google through Firebase Authentication (a Google service).
- We use them to show your name in the app and in prayer reminders, and to keep your coins safe on every device.

## 3. Purchases and coins
- You can support the app with optional in app purchases through Google Play. Google handles all payments. We never see your card or bank details.
- Each purchase gives coins that expire 6 months after purchase. We store the coin amount, product, purchase time, Google order number and purchase token.
- Signed in users: this is stored in Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud, United States) under your user ID. Guests: it is stored only on the phone.
- We use the order number to detect refunds and remove the matching coins.

## 4. Ads
- The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on the Profile screen.
- AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and general app usage to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first. You can change your choice any time under Profile > "Privacy choices for ads".
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

## 5. Data we do not collect
We do not sell your data. We do not collect contacts, photos, files, messages or health data.

## 6. Security
All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Firebase data can only be read by the signed in owner.

## 7. Keeping and deleting your data
- Coins and purchase records stay in your account until you delete it.
- **Delete inside the app:** Profile > "Delete my account". This deletes your sign in and all coins in your account right away.
- **Delete without the app:** follow the steps at https://github.com/Shaboj/App-privacy/blob/main/QiblaDeleteAccount.md
- Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's privacy policy.

## 8. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
